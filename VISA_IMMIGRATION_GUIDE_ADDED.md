## 📋 Article Contents (6 Sections + Bottom Line)

### 1. Identify Your Role & Visa Type
**3 Visa Options for Healthcare:**
- 💼 **Skilled Worker Visa** - For nurses, care workers, social workers
- 🏥 **Health and Care Visa** - Specialized route, faster processing, reduced fees
- ⏰ **Temporary Worker Visa** - Short-term assignments or locum positions

**Key Tip:** Check shortage occupation list for faster approval

### 2. Secure a Job Offer & Sponsorship
**3 Critical Steps:**
- ✓ Confirmed job offer from licensed UK employer
- ✓ Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) with unique reference number
- ✓ Employer must be UK Home Office approved

**Important Note:** CoS is critical—keep it safe throughout process

### 3. Prepare Required Documents
**6 Essential Documents:**
- 📄 Valid passport
- 📋 Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS)
- 🗣️ Proof of English proficiency (IELTS)
- ✅ Professional registration (NMC, HCPC)
- 📚 Proof of qualifications and experience
- 🩺 Tuberculosis test results (if required)

**Tip:** Keep both digital and physical copies organized

### 4. Submit Your Visa Application
**4-Step Application Process:**
1. 📱 Apply online through UK government visa portal
2. 💰 Pay visa fees and healthcare surcharge
3. 📸 Book biometric appointment for fingerprints/photos
4. ⏳ Wait 3-8 weeks for processing decision

**Processing Times:** Standard 3-8 weeks; Priority available (5 business days)

### 5. Arrival & Settling In
**4 First Steps:**
- 🎫 Receive Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) on arrival
- 🏥 Register with professional body (NMC, HCPC) if needed
- 🏦 Open UK bank account, get National Insurance number
- 👋 Attend employer orientation and induction

**Tip:** Start banking/accommodation arrangements BEFORE arrival

### 6. Additional Tips for Success
**4 Key Success Strategies:**
- 🚀 **Start Early:** Visa process can take months
- ✓ **Verify Everything:** Check employer sponsorship license online
- 📚 **Stay Current:** Maintain certifications and CPD records
- 🤝 **Use Agencies:** Consider specialized international placement agencies

### Bottom Line / Conclusion
- Structured process, manageable with proper preparation
- Secure sponsorship, register with professional body, stay organized
- Early start gives smoothest pathway
- Seek guidance from employer or visa specialist

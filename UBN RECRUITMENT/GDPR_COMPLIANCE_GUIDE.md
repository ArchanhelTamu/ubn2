## What is GDPR?

**GDPR** = General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)

A legal framework that:
- Protects personal data of UK/EU citizens
- Gives individuals rights over their personal data
- Requires organizations to be transparent about data use
- Provides heavy penalties for non-compliance (up to £20 million or 4% of revenue)

**Your website MUST comply because:**
- ✅ You collect personal data (names, emails, CVs, phone numbers)
- ✅ Your users may be UK/EU residents
- ✅ UK healthcare organizations require GDPR-compliant partners
- ✅ Non-compliance damages reputation and can result in legal action

**When you move to a live server, you'll need:**

1. **HTTPS Encryption**
   - All data transmitted securely
   - Prevents interception
   - Cost: Usually free with hosting

2. **Privacy by Design**
   - Minimize data collection
   - Purpose limitation (use only for stated purpose)
   - Data minimization (only collect what you need)

3. **Data Processing Records**
   - Document what data is processed
   - Document retention periods
   - Document security measures
   - Document purpose of processing

4. **Breach Response Plan**
   - What to do if data is compromised
   - How to notify users (72-hour rule)
   - How to notify authorities

   - If using email providers, hosting, or analytics
   - Must have Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

6. **Retention Schedules**
   - How long you keep candidate data (typically 1-2 years)
   - How long you keep employer data

## User Rights Your Policy Explains

### Right to Access
- Users can request all data you hold about them
- Must provide within 30 days
- Must be free

**How**: Contact form with data access request option

### Right to Rectification
- Users can request correction of inaccurate data
- You must correct within 30 days

**How**: Contact form with correction request

### Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
- Users can request deletion of their data
- Must delete within 30 days (with exceptions)
- Exception: Legal obligation to retain

**How**: Contact form with deletion request

### Right to Data Portability
- Users can request their data in portable format (CSV, etc.)
- Must provide within 30 days
- Enables switching between services

**How**: Contact form specifying data portability request

### Right to Object
- Users can object to processing
- You must stop processing (with exceptions)

**How**: Contact form with objection notice

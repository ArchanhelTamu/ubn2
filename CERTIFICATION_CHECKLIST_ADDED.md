## 📋 Article Contents (5 Sections + Pro Tip)

### 1. Core Certifications (6 Certifications with Checklist)
- **First Aid & CPR** - Must be current, valid 2-3 years
- **Basic Life Support (BLS)** - Essential for nurses, HCAs, care workers
- **Infection Control & Health & Safety** - Often part of employer onboarding
- **Food Safety & Hygiene** - Optional but advantageous for care home roles
- **Mental Health First Aid** - Optional, recommended for social care
- **Newborn / Pediatric Care Certification** - Required for neonatal/baby care specialists

### 2. Professional Registration (3 Key Registrations)
- **Nurse / Midwife** → Nursing & Midwifery Council (NMC) - Mandatory
- **Care Worker / Social Care Staff** → Care Quality Commission (CQC) standards
- **Allied Health Professional** → Health & Care Professions Council (HCPC)

### 3. Continuing Professional Development (CPD)
- Complete required CPD hours per year
- Keep certificates from workshops, courses, conferences
- Update skills in line with UK best practices
- Includes helpful tip about continuous learning

### 4. Additional Accreditations & Memberships (3 Options)
- **NHS Trust-approved training** - Adds credibility
- **Membership in RCN / BASW** - Enhances professional standing
- **International infant care or lactation certification** - For newborn care roles

### 5. Document Preparation & Tips (4 Action Items)
- Verify degrees and diplomas with recognized international bodies
- Translate documents if not in English (official translations only)
- Keep digital copies ready for online applications
- Check visa or work permit requirements

### Pro Tip Section
- **Key Insight:** UK employers prefer NMC registration or CQC-compliant certifications
- **Benefit:** Early completion speeds up hiring process
- **Gradient box styling** for visual emphasis
